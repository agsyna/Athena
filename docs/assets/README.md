## `demo.gif`

Currently a placeholder. Replace the file, keep the name, and both the
[root README](../../README.md) and the [recipe](../ai/RECIPE.md) pick it up with
no further edits.

The recipe is referenced by the Agora catalog, which fetches the markdown from
raw GitHub, so that one points at
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agsyna/Athena/main/docs/assets/demo.gif`
rather than a relative path. A relative link would be rewritten to a `blob` URL,
and a `blob` URL does not render as an image.

### What the recording needs to show

The map is the thing that does not come across in prose, so lead with it:

1. Highlighting a passage on a real page and starting a viva.
2. The chips populating after her orientation turn.
3. At least one chip going amber or red, and then green when she circles back.
   That recovery beat is the most persuasive moment in the product.
4. Talking over her, so the interruption is visible.

Aim for roughly 20 to 30 seconds, around 1200px wide, and keep the file under a
few megabytes so it loads on the catalog page. Captions help, since a silent
autoplaying GIF loses the half of Athena that is spoken.
